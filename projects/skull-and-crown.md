## Overview
For this project, I recreated the **Skull & Crown Trading Co.** website using **Bootstrap 5** as part of the *E36: Your Choice with Bootstrap 5* experience for ICS 314. The goal was to design a visually appealing and responsive homepage inspired by a real local brand while applying Bootstrap’s components, grid system, and color utilities.

## Features
- **Responsive Navbar:** Built with Bootstrap’s `navbar` component that adjusts across screen sizes.  
- **Hero Banner and Sections:** Used `container`, `row`, and `col` classes to align content and images dynamically.  
- **Brand Integration:** Incorporated Skull & Crown’s gold logo and black theme to maintain the store’s authentic aesthetic.  
- **Typography and Colors:** Applied Bootstrap’s color palette (`text-light`, `bg-dark`) to create contrast and highlight sections.  

## Reflection
This project helped me understand how Bootstrap 5 simplifies layout management and ensures consistency across different viewports. At first, I struggled with text visibility and background contrast, but after experimenting with Bootstrap’s color utilities and custom CSS, I achieved a clean design that matched the brand identity.
